# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This README provides an entry point to OctoAcme's project management guidance and a brief overview of how projects are run.

## Project Management Processes

OctoAcme follows a lifecycle that starts with initiation, where teams validate the business need, identify stakeholders, define measurable success criteria, and decide whether work should move into planning. Planning turns the approved initiative into a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria, estimates, dependencies, milestones, a release plan, and a documented Definition of Done.

During execution and tracking, teams deliver work through a project board and small pull requests, with daily standups, weekly delivery syncs, demos, automated testing, and regular risk updates. Risks, dependencies, and stakeholder updates are maintained through clear ownership and a risk register, with escalation from the delivery team through the PM, Product Lead, and Sponsor. Releases follow standardized pre-release checks, staged deployment and smoke testing, rollback planning, stakeholder announcements, and post-deployment verification. After sprints, releases, or incidents, retrospectives capture improvements and assign owners and timelines for follow-up actions.
